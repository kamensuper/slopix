Measured results are in PERFORMANCE.md and raw build/*.json / logs.
Guest tests run inside Slopix under QEMU; host tests do not substitute for them.
The cold-boots.json elapsed values include QMP/typing and are NOT boot-time
benchmarks. The previous release was not overwritten.

Observed failure retained, not hidden:
One earlier automated launch timed out before any kernel serial output. Its
captured CPU state was SeaBIOS real mode at CS=f000 / IP=754d, reading I/O port
0x608; no Slopix kernel entry was observed. Root cause is not established.
The interrupted suite was not counted as a pass. Five fresh default-machine
boots and the final complete suite subsequently passed with unchanged boot
arguments. Evidence: build/firmware-stall-full-test.log,
build/firmware-stall-failure-registers.txt and PNG. A transient pre-kernel stall
remains a known observation; no claim of universal firmware reliability.

Other limits:
No sound, mouse aiming, multiplayer or persistent saves/configs. No filesystem,
userspace, process isolation or general kernel heap. DOOM retains its bounded
arena/session on close. First launch initializes synchronously. Fatal engine
errors show an app error state; arbitrary C faults can still affect the kernel.
First-level gameplay tested, not every Freedoom map or a full campaign.
QEMU BIOS/PS2/std VGA only; real hardware and Docker build were not tested.
